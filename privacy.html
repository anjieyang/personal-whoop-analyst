Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Personal WHOOP Analyst is a private integration used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to WHOOP recovery, cycle, sleep, workout, and body measurement data.

The public website does not collect, store, sell, or share WHOOP data. Retrieved WHOOP data and OAuth credentials are processed and stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

The owner can revoke access at any time from WHOOP account settings or the WHOOP Developer Platform.
